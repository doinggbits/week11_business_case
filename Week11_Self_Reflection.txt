My mock interview showed me both what I can rely on and what I must fix.
What went well: My strongest answers were anchored in real projects. When asked about solving a difficult problem, I used the STAR structure and described the KPC revenue assurance platform I helped lead, with concrete results (94.8% charge capture, six compliance checks) and clear honesty that those figures came from demo data. That candour made the answer more credible. I also explained technical ideas in plain language, for example describing a false alarm as a smoke detector reacting to burnt toast, which suited a non-technical interviewer.
What needs improvement: Some answers ran too long before reaching the result. I hesitated during the live SQL and Python questions. I also gave thin detail about my internship, because I have fewer measurable outcomes from work experience than from projects.
Next steps: I will practise each STAR answer for under two minutes, leading with the result. I will rehearse SQL joins and Pandas on a timer, and prepare two or three quantified examples from my internship. Then I will record another practice session and compare it with this one.
