## The business case in brief

**The problem.** Demurrage (waiting fees for tankers that overstay in a depot) is tracked on paper gate passes and spreadsheets. An estimated **KES 45M a year** goes unbilled, and disputed invoices take **45 to 60 days** to settle.

**The solution.** An automated platform that turns gate and weighbridge records into verified, audit-ready invoices, with six compliance checks and a tamper-proof audit trail. It is already built and running on demo data.

**The financials** (KES millions, 3-year view):

| | Base case (50% recovered) | Cautious case (25% recovered) |
|---|---|---|
| Annual savings after running costs | 19.00 | 7.75 |
| 3-year net gain | 45.00 | 11.25 |
| ROI | **375%** | **94%** |
| Payback | **7.6 months** | **18.6 months** |

**The ask.** Approve KES 12M for Phase 2, released in two steps, with a decision gate at day 60 that releases the remainder only if Finance confirms savings of at least the cautious case.

**Top risks.** (1) The savings estimate is not yet proven. (2) Depot data quality and customer acceptance.

## Honest notes

- The KES 45M figure is a **modelled estimate**, not audited.
- The KES 12M one-time cost, KES 3.5M yearly running cost and the 50% and 25% recovery rates are **planning assumptions** awaiting a Finance quote.
- Platform results (94.8% charge capture, 4.2-day settlement) come from a **seeded demo dataset** and are illustrative.
- In the demo, the SAP posting is **simulated**; a live SAP connection is part of Phase 2.
